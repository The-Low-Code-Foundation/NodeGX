# CCN-007 — The person test

**State:** open; needs Richard (and a person who does not code).

The test that settles the canon, pre-registered like OWN-026: one brief, two builds watched by a non-coder — an agent
in React with the app in a browser tab, and an agent in NodeGX with Studio in the conversation. Three things measured:
whether they can find where a behaviour lives, whether they can confirm the agent did what they asked, and whether
they can make one small change themselves without the agent. Plus the one this phase adds: whether they stopped it,
when, and what happened next. The result goes on the site whichever way it comes out (DIRECTION §0's rule).
