# Brief

## What this app is

A one-page newsletter sign-up: a reader types their email and presses Subscribe, and a cloud function adds them to the mailing list with a key the browser never sees.

## Who uses it

A reader who found the newsletter's link on their phone and wants to sign up in one step. The fixture exists so that P115 measures a deploy to Cloudflare on what an agent writes.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Records of any kind (that is Fixture C)
- Sign-in
- Unsubscribing
- Being a good-looking newsletter page (HST-002: not making them good)

_Agreed in the initial scoping conversation; the full record, including what was considered and rejected, is in `docs/decisions/000-initial-scope.md`._
