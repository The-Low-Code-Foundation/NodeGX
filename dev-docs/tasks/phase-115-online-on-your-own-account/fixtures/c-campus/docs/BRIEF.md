# Brief

## What this app is

A newsletter landing page that greets a reader by the id in their link and saves their answer against that id, plus an owner page where the owner signs in to read every answer and upload one file. It is P115's Fixture C: a full-stack app graded online on Cloudflare.

## Who uses it

Readers who tap a link in a newsletter on their phone (they never sign in), and the one owner who signs in to read who answered what.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Readers signing up or signing in
- Editing or deleting answers from the page
- Sending the newsletter itself (Fixture B covers the hook to the mailing list)
- Being a good-looking page (HST-002: not making them good)
