scoped from his first one. **R1 (Richard, 2026-10-14): none of the builder's messages go into this public repo.** What

Two templates, each with a tutorial he can follow to review the app and adapt it:

## His answers, in our words (2026-10-14)

**The marketplace**

- **Sellers:** many sellers, each with a dashboard to manage their own products. He, as admin, creates, edits and
  deletes sellers and any of their products. Sellers don't sign themselves up.
- **Products:** packaged food and drinks, phones and laptops, and big items such as cars.
- **Buyers:** across African countries.
- **Payment:** as many ways as possible, because they differ from country to country.
- **Delivery:** undecided; he plans to talk to logistics firms once the app exists.

**The community app**

- **Joining:** approved members only for now; open sign-up is a later question.
- **Beyond photos, prayer requests, testimonies and events:** an offline Bible (**the King James Bible**, R9) and a
  notes section.
- **Admins:** him, then volunteer admins later.
- **The devotional:** it was published as a static blog, which is offline because its free hosting limit ran out.
  They have every devotional as HTML files and will send them later.
- **Logo:** a new one for the app, which he is making.

**Both**

- **Hosting:** apps working first; later, perhaps a small home server, and he asked whether Coolify would suit a
  non-programmer (TWO-011).
