New every morning

✓ saved for offline
It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
Lamentations 3:22–23 (KJV)

Jeremiah wrote these words while Jerusalem lay in ruins. He was not looking at a good morning. He was looking at the worst one, and he still found the mercy waiting in it.

Whatever yesterday held, today did not come to you second-hand. The mercy you need for it was made fresh, for this day and no other.

Pray: Lord, thank you that I don't have to live today on yesterday's strength.
