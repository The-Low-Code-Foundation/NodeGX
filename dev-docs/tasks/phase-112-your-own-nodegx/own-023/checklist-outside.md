# Outside checklist: AI music sharing app

The sentences in `brief.txt` are numbered in the order they appear:
S1 "Let's make a music sharing app." · S2 "I use Suno… it's all been a side thought." · S3 "You can download Suno songs… link back to Suno…" · S4 "I dunno if it should be exclusive to Suno… (link back… optional?…)" · S5 "Share playlists with friends, follow other people, like or dislike…" · S6 "If we cloned Spotify…" · S7 "The UI doesn't have to be elaborate…" · S8 "Please just build it…"

Every line is checked on a phone by using the app. You'll need two accounts (A and B) for the social lines.

1. [ ] The app opens and can be used on a phone, and you can create an account and sign in. (S1, S3: "people upload", so people have their own accounts)
2. [ ] From account A, upload an MP3 file from the phone. It shows up in the app as a song. (S3: "download Suno songs as MP3… people upload their favourite music creations")
3. [ ] While uploading or editing that song, you can give it a category such as a genre, and the song then shows that category. (S3: "properly categorise them")
4. [ ] You can add cover art to the song from an image on the phone, and the art appears wherever the song is shown. (S3: "add cover art")
5. [ ] You can add background info (free text) to the song, and anyone who opens the song can read it. (S3: "background info")
6. [ ] You can add a link back to the song's Suno page. When you open the song, tapping the link takes you to that Suno page. (S3: "include a link back to Suno in case the user wants to remix")
7. [ ] You can create a made-up artist, attach the song to that artist, and open the artist's page to see the song there. (S3: "create fake artists")
8. [ ] You can create an album under that artist, add the song to it, and open the album to see the song in it. (S3: "and albums")
9. [ ] You can upload a song made with an AI tool other than Suno, and the app doesn't block it. (S4 hesitates, so this takes S3's lean: "from Suno and other AI tools")
10. [ ] You can save a song without a remix/platform link. It saves, and its page shows no broken link. (S4: "link back to the platform to remix could be optional?", taking the lean that it's optional)
11. [ ] Tap a song to play it. You can hear it, and you can pause it and then resume from the same point. (S2: "their system for playing music is terrible", so playing has to work properly)
12. [ ] Search for a word from a song's title or its artist's name. The matching song or artist shows up in the results. (S2: "searching")
13. [ ] Filter by a category such as a genre. Only songs in that category are shown. (S2: "categorising and filtering")
14. [ ] There is a way to see songs ranked by popularity. After account B plays/likes one song more than the others, that song ranks above them. (S2: "ranking a song by popularity")
15. [ ] From account A, create a playlist, add several songs to it, and play it. (S5: "Share playlists", S6: Spotify-like)
16. [ ] Share that playlist with account B. Account B can open it, see the same songs, and play them. (S5: "Share playlists with friends")
17. [ ] From account B, find account A (the person) and follow them. A appears in B's following list. B can unfollow A too. (S5: "follow other people")
18. [ ] From account B, find a creator/artist you haven't heard before and follow them. When that creator uploads a new song, B can see it without searching for it. (S7: "find new music and follow interesting creators")
19. [ ] You can like a song and dislike a song. Each choice is saved and still shows after you leave and come back. (S5: "like or dislike songs")
20. [ ] After liking several songs from one category and disliking several from another, the songs the app suggests to you lean toward the liked category and away from the disliked one. (S5: "to learn your tastes")
21. [ ] There is a place in the app to discover songs you haven't uploaded, liked, or played yet. (S7: "ability to find new music")
22. [ ] A first-time user can do lines 11, 12, 15 and 18 without help and without getting stuck. The screens are plain, not elaborate. (S7: "the simpler the better, the most important is UX")
