# Hat D — the founder or tech lead who wants the team to build, safely

You decide what tools your company uses. You want your team to build internal tools with AI, and you're worried
about two things: company data going to a provider outside the EU, and a well-meaning colleague shipping something
that leaks a key or a customer's record. Someone has shown you three folders (K, M, R), each a music-sharing app built
from the same paragraph by three different setups, with their transcripts, two graders' reports each, and what
happened when a later agent made four changes.

Answer, citing files, lines and screenshots. Verify from the folders; don't take any README's word for it.

1. For each app: where does the data live, who hosts it, and would a second user on another device see it?
2. After the key change: where did the key end up in each app? Show the search that proves it. Would your team have
   caught a leak?
3. Who can read and write what, in each app? Is there a file or a screen that says so in words a non-developer reads?
4. From what each folder shows, could a European model have built and run it? Say what you can verify and what you
   can't.
5. Who maintains each one in a year: what licence, what has to stay running, what would a new agent need to know, and
   is that written in the folder?
6. What would a leak look like in each, and which setup, if any, would you let your team start with? Say why in
   terms of the artefacts.
