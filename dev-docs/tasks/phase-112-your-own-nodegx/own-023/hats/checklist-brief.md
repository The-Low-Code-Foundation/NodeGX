# Brief for the stranger who writes the outside checklist

You are given one paragraph in `brief.txt`: what a person asked for. Write a checklist of the things that person
would do on their phone to find out whether the app does what they asked. One line per thing, in the order they'd
try them, each line something a grader can mark pass or fail by using the app, not by reading its code. Take the
paragraph's own lean where it hesitates, and say in the line which sentence of the paragraph it comes from. Don't
add things the paragraph didn't ask for. Don't assume how the app is built.
