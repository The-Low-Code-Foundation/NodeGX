# Brief

## What this app is

A one-person habit tracker: add a habit, tick it off for today, see the last 7 days and the current streak for each habit, and delete a habit you no longer want. Everything is kept on this device and is still there next time.

## Who uses it

One person, on their own device — the person who asked for it. They open it once or twice a day: tick off today's habits, glance at the streaks, add a habit when they think of one, delete one they have given up on.

## Deliberately out of scope

- No accounts, sign-in or profiles — it is one person's list on one device.
- No syncing or sharing between devices or people.
- No reminders or notifications.
- No partial credit: a day is done or it is not.
- No habit schedules or targets (every day only; no '3 times a week').
- No history beyond the last 7 days and no charts or statistics beyond the streak count.
- No renaming a habit — delete it and add it again.
