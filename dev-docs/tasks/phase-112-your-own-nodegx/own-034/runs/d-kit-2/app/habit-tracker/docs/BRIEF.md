# Brief

## What this app is

A single-person habit tracker: add habits, tick each one off for today, see the last seven days and a streak for each, delete a habit. Everything is kept on this device and is there again next time.

## Who uses it

One person on their own phone or laptop, opening the app once or twice a day to mark off a handful of habits (maybe three to ten) and glance at how the week and the streak are going. They expect yesterday's ticks to still be there, and they expect to do it in a few seconds without signing in.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Editing or renaming a habit once it exists
- Reordering habits
- Showing anything older than the last seven days (the data keeps it; the screen shows seven)
- Accounts, sharing or syncing between devices
- Undoing a delete
- Partial credit or quantities (three of five glasses)
- Reminders or notifications
