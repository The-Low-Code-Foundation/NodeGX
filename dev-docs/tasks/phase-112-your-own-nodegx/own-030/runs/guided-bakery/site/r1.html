Our Story: Baked with Love in Lyon

Welcome to Lyon Family Bakery. Our passion for baking is rooted deeply in the heart of Lyon, a city renowned for its culinary heritage. We started this bakery with a simple, heartfelt desire: to share the warmth and tradition of homemade bread and exquisite pastries with our local community.

Every loaf of sourdough, every flaky croissant, and every delicate cake is crafted with time-honored techniques and the finest ingredients. We believe that the best moments are shared over delicious, freshly baked goods, connecting generations through the simple pleasure of good food.

We are a family bakery, dedicated to bringing authentic, comforting flavors to the people of Lyon. From our ovens to your table, we pour our love and expertise into every creation. Come experience the tradition and taste the difference that family care makes.
