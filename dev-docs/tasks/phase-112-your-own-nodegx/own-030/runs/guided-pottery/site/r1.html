About

This portfolio showcases handmade bowls crafted with intention. The philosophy behind the work centers on the tactile experience of pottery and the beauty found in simple, functional forms.

Through weekend classes, the process is shared—offering a hands-on introduction to the craft for those seeking a mindful, creative escape.
