# The brief, in Richard's words (2026-10-30)

> "I feel like to test this out, I'd rather design a new phase where we let Opus take a crack at making the game with our
> new harness obliging it to respect the canon, making a game from scratch and then letting the user decide what part they
> want to edit, being purposefully pedantic and annoying, and seeing if a follow up Opus session can handle the heat.
>
> For the game, let's do a ripoff of Fallout Shelter. I really loved the idea of base building, resource management,
> community management, social politics and sending NPCs out on expeditions. Have a think about a fun spinoff, we'll use
> three.js but use the cutaway concept with 3D rooms but looking like a 2D platformers. Let's plan to let the player
> control the NPCs optionally when they go out on an expedition, with platformer fighting mechanics fending off irradiated
> monsters in the wasteland to scavenge and find supplies and food. I dunno what 3D models to use for the people, something
> where you can get to hundreds without overloading a consumer laptop or Windows tablet. Have a think, do some research and
> scope up a prototype in task docs please."

## What is ruled by the brief

- Opus 5.5 builds it, from scratch, under the harness. Not us.
- The harness obliges the canon (P129 draft 2): the latent layer always, no editor until asked.
- The owner asks for edits, pedantic on purpose. A follow-up Opus session handles them.
- Fallout Shelter's bones: base building, resources, community, politics, expeditions.
- three.js; the cutaway (3D rooms, front face cut, seen from the side); reads like a 2D platformer.
- Expeditions can be played: platformer fighting against irradiated monsters; scavenge supplies and food.
- Hundreds of people on a consumer laptop or a Windows tablet (the tablet in the house is a Surface Pro 2017: Core m3,
  4 GB, HD 615, Windows 10, Edge or Chrome).

## What the brief leaves open (the README's rulings)

The setting, the audience, death, the people route, one arm or two, who plays the pedant, where the lab lives, the title.

## What this phase will not do

- Build the game ourselves. If Opus cannot, that is the finding.
- Multiplayer. Saving to a backend. Mobile.
- Polish past what the rounds ask for. Richard's Life Sim rule: *"realism can come much later once we get the game
  mechanics right"*.
- Claim anything on the site before THE-TEST.md's measures are in.

## The owner's device

Richard's Mac for the build rounds; the Surface Pro 2017 for the people probe and at least one play round. Every headless
drive runs at the device's pixel ratio and window (castle canon rule 4).
