# IG-008 — The kids' verdict, in their words, the same day

**Opened 2026-09-28.** **Status: ⬜ not started.** Depends on IG-001–IG-007 built into the Windows installer
(P105 CG-008's workflow). No lane: Richard and the kids. Replaces P105's CG-009 (never run) on this build.

## 1. The person sentence

> **Two children play the island on their tablet for as long as they like, say what they think, and their
> words, not our clauses, decide what the next phase builds.**

## 2. What it is

- Richard installs the build on the tablet and plays it with each child, in French, band 7–9 and band 10–12.
- What is watched, not asked: does the younger one use Drive before Teach without being told; does either
  pan the island to find a working robot; does the older one build `if Olive read`; does either ask for a
  robot she does not have; how long a frame stutter or an Olive wait is tolerated; when they stop and why;
  what they customise first (a robot's name, a hat, a colour).
- What is asked, afterwards, once: "what would you change?" and "what was the best bit?"
- Their words go into the UNTRACKED kids' notes the same day, verbatim; this file carries only what changes
  and why, with no names.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. Both children have played at least one request with Drive, one fetch loop (water or stones), and, for
   the older one, one Olive read, on the tablet, offline.
2. The tablet's readings (IG-007 AC3 frame time, the P105 CG-008 Olive timings) are compared with what the
   children tolerated; a wait that made a child leave the screen re-ranks the thing that caused it.
3. Their verdicts are in the untracked notes, dated; this file lists the changes they imply as rows on the
   README board, or says "no changes".
4. One observed reaction each for: Drive, the fold, a robot seen working from the island, an Olive read.

## 4. Traps

Kids' names in a public repo (a test account was once named after one and scrubbed before a push). A relayed
conclusion decays faster than a relayed measurement: write what they did, then what they said.
