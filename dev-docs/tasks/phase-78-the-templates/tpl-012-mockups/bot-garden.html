For grown-ups

Olive, offline

Olive is the helper owl. Her hints come from the game's own rules. A small language model, stored on this computer, only puts them into friendly words and writes the islanders' thank-yous.

Where Olive lives

This computer · small model (0.5 GB)Installed. Slow but private: a hint takes 5–10 seconds here.
Family Mac · bigger model (1.3 GB)Not found on the home network right now. Olive uses this computer.
No model at allThe game still works. Olive's hints use her written lines instead.

What Olive is allowed to do

  • Say a hint in her own words. The hint itself is decided by the game, not by the model.
  • Write a thank-you or a greeting for an islander, in French or English, under 25 words.
  • Never place a block, never solve a request, never talk about anything outside the island.
  • Every line is checked against a word list before it is shown. A refused line falls back to a written one.

Nothing leaves the house

  • No account, no internet, no adverts. The game is installed like any other program.
  • Saved on this computer. A save code moves a garden to another computer.
  • No timers, streaks, points or "you missed a day". Rewards are cosmetic and always earnable.
