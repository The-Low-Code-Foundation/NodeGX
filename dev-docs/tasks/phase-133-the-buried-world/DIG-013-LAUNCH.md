# DIG-013 — Launch to the NodeGX community

**Phase:** [133](README.md). **Depends on:** all. **Round:** 13. **Scope:** [SCOPE §10](SCOPE.md). **Ruling:** R6
(open sign-up).

> "I want this to be perfect and launch it to the NodeGX community" — Richard, 2026-11-03

## What

- **A stranger's first five minutes:** someone who has never heard of it lands, signs up, walks out of the hangar,
  pulses, digs, finds something, and understands the todo half without being told to use it. Watched, not assumed.
- **Wording** against SCOPE §10: one description used everywhere; none of the unsafe phrases; Help carries "not
  therapy, not medical advice" and crisis lines copied from the providers' own pages (Betr's `helplines.js` is MIT and
  dated; reuse it with its dates checked again).
- **Phones:** the iPhone, an older iPhone, an Android; frame rate in a town and during a pulse.
- **The page on nodegx.io** (push only its own folder; the changelog lives on the site), saying plainly what is built in
  NodeGX (the todo app, the backend) and what is not (the game is hand-written three.js).
- **The post** for the community: Richard's to send.

## Acceptance

- **AC1.** Two strangers' first five minutes, observed, with what confused them fixed.
- **AC2.** A wording gate over every page and string fails on the unsafe phrases.
- **AC3.** The three phones measured; any below 30 fps in a town gets the lite settings by default.
- **AC4.** Richard reads the page and the post and says go.
