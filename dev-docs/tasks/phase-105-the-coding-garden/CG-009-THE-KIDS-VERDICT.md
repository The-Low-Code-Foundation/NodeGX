# CG-009 — The kids' verdict, in their words, the same day

**Opened 2026-09-27.** **Status: ⬜ not started.** Depends on CG-008. No lane: Richard and the kids.

## 1. The person sentence

> **Two children play the garden on their tablet for as long as they like, say what they think, and their
> words — not our clauses — decide what the next session builds.**

## 2. What it is

- Richard installs CG-008's build on the tablet and plays it with each child, in French, band 7–9 and
  band 10–12.
- What is watched, not asked: does the younger one reach the fold without being told; does the older one
  laugh or sigh at rung 4 (Olive gives one block); does either ask Olive something the slots do not
  allow (that is the pressure on the fence, and the answer is a word list entry, not a text box); how
  long a request takes; when they stop and why; what they customise first.
- What is asked, afterwards, once: "what would you change?" and "what was the best bit?"
- Their words go into the UNTRACKED kids' notes the same day, verbatim; this file carries only what
  changes in the game and why, with no names.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. Both children have played at least one coding request and one Olive rung, on the tablet, offline.
2. Their verdicts are recorded verbatim in the untracked notes, dated, and this file lists the changes
   they imply as new rows on the README board (or says "no changes").
3. The tablet's timings (CG-008 AC4) are compared with what the children tolerated: if a rung's wait
   made a child leave the screen, that rung is re-ranked or cached.
4. The Predict step, the fold and the exam gate each have one observed reaction written here.

## 4. Traps

Kids' names in a public repo (a test account was once named after one and scrubbed before a push); a
relayed conclusion decays faster than a relayed measurement — write what they did, then what they said.
