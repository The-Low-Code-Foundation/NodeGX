# HST-009 — The words

**Opened 2026-10-07.** **Depends on HST-008, P111 SEE-008.** **Status: 📋 not started.** Kind: copy and distribution.

## 1. The person sentence

> **Someone about to start a website with Claude reads one sentence, says it to Claude, and from then on the connector
> explains what it is doing in words they understand, including how they would leave.**

## 2. Why

README §1 reading 8: the step where a person hears about NodeGX is the whole business and it is not code. The
ex-client found everything else by reading guides. The sentence has to be short enough to be repeated by someone who
does not know what a framework is, and true (P111 SEE-009 binds every claim on nodegx.io to a gate).

## 3. What to do

1. **The sentence.** Drafted against HST-000's list and HST-008's numbers, and only claiming what the twelve runs showed.
   The pitch is the exit and the restraint, not the framework (README §9).
2. **The narration.** Everything the connector says while it works, in the person's words, kept as copy and passed
   through the content-authoring skill. Grade: a stranger reads only the narration from a run and can say what they own
   and what they would do to leave.
3. **The listing.** What Anthropic's connectors directory needs (a Team or Enterprise organisation, review, read
   2026-10-07) against what the open MCP registry and a `.mcpb` bundle need. Both costs in a table for R4.
4. **The exit page.** One page on nodegx.io: what you own, where it is, how to leave, in the same words.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The sentence is on nodegx.io with its gate (SEE-009's mechanism), and it is under twenty words.
2. The stranger's reading of the narration names what they own and how to leave, without prompting.
3. R4 is asked with both costs measured.

## 5. Not this task

Marketing beyond the one page. A video. Anyone's logo.
