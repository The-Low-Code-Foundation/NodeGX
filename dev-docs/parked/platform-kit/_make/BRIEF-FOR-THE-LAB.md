# The brief

> For the game, let's do a ripoff of Fallout Shelter. I really loved the idea of base building, resource management,
> community management, social politics and sending NPCs out on expeditions. Have a think about a fun spinoff, we'll use
> three.js but use the cutaway concept with 3D rooms but looking like a 2D platformers. Let's plan to let the player
> control the NPCs optionally when they go out on an expedition, with platformer fighting mechanics fending off irradiated
> monsters in the wasteland to scavenge and find supplies and food. I dunno what 3D models to use for the people, something
> where you can get to hundreds without overloading a consumer laptop or Windows tablet.

## The first play (what round one must show)

A tower with four dry floors and the dock: generator, hydroponics, desalinator, dorm, and twelve dwellers walking and
working; the cutaway readable at two zooms with the waterline across it; one incident (rats); one expedition to one level
(the department store's top floors) with auto and with control; one assembly question. Fun before breadth: Richard plays
and writes the list.

The people in `assets/people/` (Microsoft Rocketbox, MIT) are the ones to use.
