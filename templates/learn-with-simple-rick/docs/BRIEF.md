# Brief

## What this app is

A chat app for one coach and his learners: Rick's lessons arrive as a thread of short clips and messages, one at a time, cut from his screencasts by a local studio, and the learner replies in the same thread. A second app is the coach's inbox, with notes, objectives and sessions only he sees.

## Who uses it

Learners (English and French, on their phone first, installed as a PWA) who open a conversation Rick sent them, watch a clip, tap, paste, and tell him what happened. Richard (Rick), on his phone, who reads their messages and replies, keeps private notes, objectives and session records per learner. The studio on Richard's Mac publishes threads over a staff key.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Not a SaaS: one teacher, his learners, no sign-up page.
- Not a course: no concepts table, no learning path, no programme, no catalogue.
- Not a page: anything that needs a new surface to understand is out.
- No automated messages to learners: the system reminds Rick, Rick writes.
- No model in the cloud except Claude mode's one function.
- Nothing is ever deleted.
