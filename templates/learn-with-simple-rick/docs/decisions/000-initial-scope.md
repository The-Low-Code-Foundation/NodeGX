# 000 — Initial scope

_Recorded 2026-10-12T12:54:41.590Z, from the scoping conversation held before this project was created._

## What was asked for

> I need a teacher interface where I can see all learners, drill into their conversations etc. Add new learners, generate links to share, OTP login only using their email, PWA from the start, super easy interface for learners, a bit more complex for me to take notes on people, assign them objectives only I can see, make notes inside each conversation that they can't see. — "eLearning + coaching concepts, but disguised as a chat platform." — "The same UI as every other chat app they use with some twists = happy users"

## What was decided

**The app.** A chat app for one coach and his learners: Rick's lessons arrive as a thread of short clips and messages, one at a time, cut from his screencasts by a local studio, and the learner replies in the same thread. A second app is the coach's inbox, with notes, objectives and sessions only he sees.

**Who it is for.** Learners (English and French, on their phone first, installed as a PWA) who open a conversation Rick sent them, watch a clip, tap, paste, and tell him what happened. Richard (Rick), on his phone, who reads their messages and replies, keeps private notes, objectives and session records per learner. The studio on Richard's Mac publishes threads over a staff key.

**Pages.**

- Thread — The learner's conversation: one message at a time, the reply bar at the bottom, the conversations drawer, the summary block and reset (LOOK.md 1–13).
- Sign in — Email, then the six-digit code.
- Privacy — What is stored, what goes to Anthropic and how it is aliased (R3), in Richard's words.
- Inbox — Coach: learners sorted by who is waiting on Rick.
- Learner — Coach: notes, objectives, sessions, conversations, the share link.
- Coach thread — Coach: the conversation as the learner sees it, coach notes in a surface that cannot be mistaken for the reply bar, Claude mode.

**Records.**

- Learner — One person Rick coaches. The learner reads their own row except the coach-only fields. — user (the _User it signs in as), email, name, language, prefs (theme, font, size), aliases (the find-and-replace table for anything sent to Anthropic; coach-only), inviteToken, package, meetCode (their recurring Google Meet code), notes (coach-only free text) — has many Conversation; has many CoachNote, Objective, Session (coach-only)
- Conversation — One thread: a lesson, a session review or a follow-up. — learner, title, kind (lesson | session-review | follow-up), state (open | done | archived), language, dubbed, linkedFrom, summary, dueBefore, shareable, run — belongs to Learner; has many Message; may link from another Conversation
- Message — One bubble in a run of a conversation. — conversation, run, seq, from (rick | learner), origin (cut | typed | drafted | dictated), kind (text | clip | choice | question | paste | learner), body, clip {url, start, end}, options, revealsAfter — belongs to Conversation
- Progress — How far the learner has revealed a run. — learner, conversation, run, lastSeq, lastOpened
- CoachNote — Rick's private note on a learner, a conversation or a message. Never shown to the learner. — learner, conversation, message, text, on
- Objective — A goal Rick holds for a learner. Coach-only. — learner, text, setOn, doneOn
- Session — A coaching session: Rick's prediction, what happened, the comparison. Coach-only. — learner, at, recordingDriveId, predicted, happened, comparison, reviewConversation, reminderOn
- PushSubscription — Where to send 'Rick replied'. — learner, endpoint, keys

**Backend.** A NodeGX backend on the Hetzner box at training.digitalbricks.io. Sign-in by a six-digit code by email (SRK-001). Learners read their own rows through path rules; the coach-only fields are hidden per field; every learner write goes through a cloud function; staff (role:staff) and the studio's staff key write everything. Nothing is ever deleted; a reset is a new run.

**Rules for this project.**

- Every learner-facing sentence passes the content-authoring skill's offline checks.
- Colours and spacing through tokens, never raw hex or px.
- delete: nobody on every collection; a reset is a new run.
- Text sent to Anthropic is built from rows and passed through Learner.aliases first.

## What was considered and rejected

This is the section with the longest shelf life. It is what stops the next reader — or the next
assistant — helpfully rebuilding something that was deliberately left out.

- **A lesson page with the clips laid out as steps** — rejected: Richard: "Blog post. I'm already bored […] I'm overwhelmed already" (first mockup).
- **Intake and programme generated by Claude (the DBT template)** — rejected: "one of my big mistakes with Digital Bricks Training was trying to offload intake and programme development to Claude"
- **Branches by platform or level** — rejected: "People don't like choices in general" — choices are inconsequential and personal only.
- **Magic-link sign-in** — rejected: A link from a phone's mail app opens the browser, not the installed PWA; a six-digit code (R5).
- **Learners write rows directly** — rejected: A learner could post a message marked from Rick; every learner write is a cloud function.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Not a SaaS: one teacher, his learners, no sign-up page.
- Not a course: no concepts table, no learning path, no programme, no catalogue.
- Not a page: anything that needs a new surface to understand is out.
- No automated messages to learners: the system reminds Rick, Rick writes.
- No model in the cloud except Claude mode's one function.
- Nothing is ever deleted.
