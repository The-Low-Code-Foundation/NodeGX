# Brief

## What this app is

A 1:1 coaching platform: each learner follows a plan of live sessions, homework and check-ins that their coach controls, hands in work in any form, keeps a portfolio of what they made, and can message the coach at any time. The coach runs everything from one inbox.

## Who uses it

Learners: trainers being coached 1:1 in AI and NodeGX, who want to know what's next, hand in work and ask questions. Coach: Richard, who builds each learner's plan, reviews hand-ins and answers messages from one inbox.

## Deliberately out of scope

- Cohorts and groups
- Payments
- Built-in calendar booking (a booking link is enough)
- AI lessons
- Draft with Claude in the inbox
- Employer reports
- A mobile app
- Multiple coaches
- Scores or grades: review is approve or ask for changes

_Agreed in the initial scoping conversation; the full record, including what was considered and rejected, is in `docs/decisions/000-initial-scope.md`._
