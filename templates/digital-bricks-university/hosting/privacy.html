Privacy

Your data

Version 2026-11-12

What Digital Bricks University holds about you, why, where it lives, and how to have it or have it deleted. It is short, and it is written to be read.

Who runs it

Digital Bricks University is run by Digital Bricks (Richard Osborne), who is also your coach. Digital Bricks decides what is held and why, and is the one to write to about any of it.

Your account

Your email address and your name, and, if your coach adds them, your organisation, your job title and your time zone (so dates and reminders are in your time). There is no password: to sign in you ask for a link, and we email you one that works once and lasts fifteen minutes. That browser then stays signed in until you sign out, so sign out on a computer you share. We also note when you last opened the course.

Your plan

The sessions, homework and check-ins your coach planned for you, with their dates, and how far along you are.

What you hand in

Every version of every piece of homework you hand in: the files you attach (up to 50 MB each), the links and notes you write, and when. Your coach's review of each version is kept with it: whether it is approved or needs changes, and the note they wrote you.

My creations

The things you choose to keep in your portfolio: files, links and interactive pages, with the titles and notes you give them. A page you upload opens in a closed frame, apart from the rest of the course. You can delete anything there yourself.

Check-ins

Your answers to the short check-ins your coach sends, and when you sent them.

Messages with your coach

Every message either of you writes, what on your plan it was about, when it was written, and whether the other person has read it. When you write, we email your coach, and the email carries your message. When they reply, we email you, and the email carries their reply, unless you turn those emails off: every one of them has a link that stops them in one press. That link never stops your sign-in links.

Your coach's notes

Your coach can keep private notes about your coaching, to prepare your sessions. They are about you, so they are your data too: you can ask for them like everything else here, and they are deleted with your account. The course also notes when you have gone quiet for a while, so your coach can check in.

Who can see it

You can see your plan, your hand-ins and their reviews, your creations, your check-in answers and your messages. Your coach can see all of it, and their notes. No other learner can see any of it. The person who runs the server can reach the database to keep it working, and does not read it for anything else.

Why we hold it

To coach you. That is the only reason. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, use it to build a profile of you, or send any of it to an AI model.

Where it lives

On one server we rent from Hetzner, in Falkenstein, Germany, with an encrypted copy in Hetzner's storage in Nuremberg, Germany (see the next section). One other company is involved: Brevo, an email service based in France, sends our emails, so it sees your email address and what each email says. That is your sign-in links, an email when your coach replies to you (carrying their reply), an email when a check-in is waiting for you, and, to your coach, an email carrying the message you sent. Apart from that and the encrypted copies, nothing leaves the server.

Copies we keep in case something breaks

Every night the server takes a copy of the database and of the files you handed in, and keeps the last seven. It also encrypts that night's copy and stores it in Hetzner's storage in Nuremberg, so that losing the server does not lose your work. The key that opens those copies is not on the server, and Hetzner does not have it. The stored copies are deleted after seven days, the same as the ones on the server.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are being coached here. When you ask us to delete your account, or your coaching ends and you ask for it to go, we delete your account and everything listed here within a week of your asking, and tell you when it is done. The nightly copies still hold it until they age out, within seven days after that. Brevo keeps its own record of the emails it sent you, under its own rules.

What you can do about it

Ask for a copy of everything we hold about you, ask for something to be corrected, or ask for all of it to be deleted. Write to the address below; there is no form to fill in and no reason to give.

Getting in touch

Write to richard+dbuniversity@digitalbricks.io. A person reads it. If you are not happy with how we handled your data, you have the right to complain to your national data-protection authority.

When this changes

If anything that matters changes (a new kind of data, a new company handling it, a different period), the version at the top changes, and we tell you by email before it applies.
